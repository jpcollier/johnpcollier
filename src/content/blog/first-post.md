This is the first post on a new site. I've been meaning to write more consistently for a while — partly as a way to think through ideas, partly because I spend a lot of time reading things that deserve more than a fleeting mental note.

The plan is to keep things loose. Short posts when something is rattling around in my head. Longer pieces when a topic warrants it. Notes on books and papers I've been working through.

No promises about frequency. More of a when-I-have-something-to-say situation.
